04Getting here

Just outside Flúðir in Hrunamannahreppur, South Iceland, on the Golden Circle. The shops and restaurants of Flúðir are about 1 km away by road.

Source: Airbnb listings; OSRM road distance

About 140 km by road, over the Hellisheiði pass on Route 1. Allow around two hours, more in winter.

Source: OSRM road route, 22 Oct 2026

About 104 km by road, about 1 hour 30 minutes, over Hellisheiði on Route 1 and past Selfoss.

Source: OSRM road route

By road from the houses: Geysir about 26 km, Gullfoss about 33 km and Þingvellir about 66 km.

Source: OSRM road routes

About 2.4 km by road. Mountain Villa’s listing puts it at a 13-minute walk or a 4-minute drive.

Source: Mountain Villa’s Airbnb listing; OSRM

In Flúðir, a few minutes away: a grocery store, a liquor store, a pharmacy and restaurants. Greenhouses in the area sell their own produce and baked goods.

Source: Both Airbnb listings

Check the roads on umferdin.is or call 1777 before you set out, and read the alerts on safetravel.is. The road from Reykjavík and the airport crosses Hellisheiði, a mountain pass on Route 1.

Source: umferdin.is, safetravel.is (read 4 Nov 2026)

05Winter

Yes, on dark nights with clear skies. Guests have written about seeing them from both houses in September, January and February. They are never guaranteed: the Icelandic Met Office forecasts cloud cover and aurora activity on vedur.is.

Source: Guest reviews; vedur.is

From about mid-August to late April, when the sun goes at least 12 degrees below the horizon at night. From late April to mid-August the nights stay too light.

Source: Computed for 64.13° N

About 4 hours on 21 December: the sun rises at 11:15 and sets at 15:23 at the houses. By 21 January it is about 6 hours, and about 9 by 21 February.
