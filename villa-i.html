a log house with a glass sky

A beautiful cabin on a large private property near Flúðir. Family friendly, with two cribs, a play pen, two high chairs and a changing table, and puzzles, board games, books and great Wi-Fi for the slow evenings.

Outside, the newly built spa: a hot tub, a cold plunge, an outdoor shower and a sauna with a large window over the Icelandic countryside. Children have the swing; groups have a game of frisbee golf.
