a cabin on a large private property

A beautiful cabin on a large private property near Flúðir. Family friendly, with two cribs, a play pen, two high chairs and a changing table, and puzzles, board games, books and great Wi-Fi for the slow evenings.

Outside, the newly built spa: a hot tub, a cold plunge, an outdoor shower and a sauna with a large window over the Icelandic countryside. Children have the swing; groups have a game of frisbee golf.

everything for a long stay.

  • The spa

    Hot tub, cold plunge, outdoor shower and a sauna with a window onto the fields.

  • The kitchen

    An induction stovetop, a grill on the patio, an Instant Pot and a Nespresso machine. Crystal glasses and stoneware.

  • For families

    Two cribs, a play pen, high chairs, a changing table, and a swing outside.

  • To keep moving

    An exercise bike, free weights and yoga mats, then a dip in the hot tub.

the rooms

Four bedrooms with king beds, a sofa bed in the living room and a crib in the fourth: room for ten without anyone on a floor. Timber walls and ceilings throughout, and windows onto the fields from every side.
