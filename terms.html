House rules and booking terms

The same rules for every guest. Sending a request means the person who books accepts them for the whole group.

Draft for Hafþór and Kelsey to confirm before the site goes live.

01Who you book with

  • Mountain Villa and Mountain Villa II are rented out by Fjallið Hafþór ehf., kt. 440718-0190, Asparlundi 2, 210 Garðabær, Iceland. Your hosts are Hafþór Júlíus Björnsson and Kelsey.
  • Questions before or during your stay: +354 822 0377 or breyn@simnet.is.

02How booking works

  • Choose your dates and send a request. A request is free and does not hold the house.
  • The hosts reply within 24 hours. If the dates are free they confirm the price and send a secure link to pay.
  • The house is yours once the payment has arrived. Dates that are not paid within 48 hours of the link are released.
  • Prices are in euros and include VAT, accommodation tax and cleaning. Nothing is charged on this website.

03Cancelling

  • 30 days or more before arrival: full refund.
  • 14 to 29 days before arrival: half refunded.
  • Less than 14 days before arrival, a no show or leaving early: no refund.
  • If the hosts have to cancel, you get everything back.
  • If the Icelandic Road Administration closes the road to Flúðir on your arrival day, the hosts move your stay to new dates. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
  • The 14 day right of withdrawal for distance purchases does not apply to accommodation booked for specific dates (Icelandic Act no. 16/2016 on consumer contracts).

04Guests

  • Each house sleeps up to 10 people, children included. Both houses together sleep 20. Mention babies under two in your request.
  • Only the guests in the booking stay overnight. Day visitors need the hosts' permission.
  • The person who books must be at least 25, stay at the house and accept these terms for everyone in the group.

05Arrival and departure

  • Check-in from 16:00, checkout by 11:00. Early arrival or late checkout only by arrangement.
  • Self check-in with a lockbox. The code and directions arrive the day before you do.

06House rules

  • No parties or events.
  • No pets.
  • No smoking or vaping inside the houses or on the decks.
  • Quiet from 22:00 to 08:00. Sound carries far in the countryside.
  • Barbecues outdoors only. No open fires.
  • Leave the house as you found it: dishes washed and rubbish in the bins outside.

07The spa decks

  • The hot tub, cold plunge and sauna are used at your own risk.
  • Children use them only with an adult beside them, every time.
  • No glass in or around the hot tub. The kitchen has outdoor glasses for the deck.
  • Shower before the hot tub and put its cover back after use.
  • Do not use the sauna or the cold plunge after drinking alcohol. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition, ask your doctor first.

08Damage

  • The person who books is responsible for damage beyond normal wear during the stay. Tell the hosts about any breakage straight away.
  • Repairs and replacements are charged at cost, with photos and receipts.
  • A party, more guests than booked or other serious breaks of these rules can end the stay without a refund.

09Safety

  • There are security cameras on the outside of the property. There are none inside the houses.
  • Both houses have smoke alarms. In an emergency call 112.
  • In winter, check umferdin.is for road conditions and safetravel.is before you drive.

10Your details

  • Your name, email and phone are used only to handle your booking. They are never sold, and shared only with the payment provider to take your payment.
  • They are kept as long as Icelandic bookkeeping law requires. To see or delete what is kept about you, write to breyn@simnet.is.
  • These terms follow Icelandic law.
